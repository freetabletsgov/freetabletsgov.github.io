How to reach us

Email

This is the best way to reach the editorial team.

support@freetabletsgov.github.io

Response times

We aim to review and respond to messages within a few business days. We cannot respond to questions about your personal eligibility or application status.

What we can and cannot help with

We can help with

  • General questions about content on this site
  • Requests for clarifications or corrections
  • Suggestions for new guides or topics
  • Reporting outdated provider details or links

We cannot help with

  • Checking your application status
  • Uploading documents for you
  • Expediting approvals or shipments
  • Replacing or repairing your device
  • Any government-related processing

Email safety

Please do not send sensitive documents or personal numbers by email. For official application steps, always use your provider’s secure portal.

We maintain this site as an educational resource only. To apply for benefits or upload documents, contact your chosen provider directly.

Important disclaimer

Free Tablets Gov Guide is not a government office and does not process any applications. We provide educational content to help readers understand common rules, documents, and provider options.
