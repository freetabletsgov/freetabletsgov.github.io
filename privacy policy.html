Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains what information Free Tablets Gov Guide collects when you visit the site, how we use it, and what choices you have. The site is an educational resource and does not run tablet or phone applications.

Last updated: October 20, 2025

Who we are

Free Tablets Gov Guide is an independent educational website that publishes information about tablet and connectivity support programs in the United States. We are not a government agency and we do not process or approve any applications.

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy you can email us at support@freetabletsgov.github.io.

Information we collect

This site is designed to collect as little personal information as reasonably possible. In normal use, we collect the following limited types of information.

Technical and usage data

When you visit the site, standard technical information may be recorded automatically by our hosting provider or basic analytics tools. This can include your IP address, browser type, pages viewed, and the time of your visit. This information is used to keep the site secure and to understand general traffic patterns.

Information you choose to send

If you email us directly, we will receive the email address and any information that you include in your message. We use this information only to read and respond to your message or to improve the content on the site.

How we use information

We use the limited information described above for the following purposes.

  • To operate and maintain the website and keep it secure
  • To understand which pages readers find most useful so we can improve them
  • To reply to emails that you send to us
  • To detect abuse or technical issues that could harm the site or its visitors

We do not use your information to build marketing profiles and we do not sell your personal information.

Cookies and analytics

The site may use simple cookies or similar technologies to keep the site secure and to understand basic traffic patterns, such as how many visitors read a particular page. Cookies are small text files stored on your device.

You can usually control cookies through your browser settings. If you block or delete cookies, the core educational content on the site should still be accessible, although some functions may work less smoothly.

Third party links and referrals

Many pages on Free Tablets Gov Guide link to third party websites, such as federal agencies, state regulators, nonprofit groups, or providers. These sites have their own privacy policies and data practices that may be different from ours.

If you follow a link to a third party site, their terms and privacy rules apply. We encourage you to review those policies before submitting any personal information to them.

How we protect information

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect the site and the limited information that we handle. This includes using reputable hosting, keeping software up to date, and limiting access to email accounts that receive contact messages.

No website or email system is perfectly secure. You should avoid sending sensitive information, such as full Social Security numbers or detailed benefits letters, through email. Use official provider or government portals for applications and document uploads.

Children's privacy

Free Tablets Gov Guide is written for adults who are applying for benefits for themselves or their households. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent personal information to us, contact us and we will delete it as soon as reasonably possible.

Your choices and rights

Because this site collects very limited information, your main choices are straightforward.

  • You can decide whether to send us an email or not
  • You can ask us to delete an email you sent, where possible, by contacting us
  • You can use browser settings to control or block cookies

If you live in a region with specific privacy rights, such as the European Union or some U.S. states, those rights are respected in line with applicable law, subject to the limited data that we actually hold.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in how the site operates or in applicable laws. When we make changes, we will update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the site after an update means that you accept the revised policy.

How to contact us about privacy

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or about how we handle information, you can email the editorial team.

Email: support@freetabletsgov.github.io

Important disclaimer

Free Tablets Gov Guide is an independent educational site. We are not the government and we do not process or approve any benefits. For official rules and personal cases, always contact program administrators or providers directly.
